Privacy Policy

SmartX LTE respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how the SmartX LTE mobile application collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use our app to access your milk-related data and activities available through the SmartX LTE web portal.

1. Information Collection and Use

The SmartX LTE mobile app provides users with access to selected features of the SmartX LTE web portal, including milk-related data, records, and activity information. To provide these services, we may collect or process the following information:

  • User Information: Information such as your name, registered mobile number, email address, or user ID, as applicable to your account.
  • Milk-Related Data: Milk collection records, quantity details, quality information, transaction records, and other milk-related information available through your account.
  • Activity Information: Records of activities, transactions, and other account-related information displayed through the SmartX LTE platform.
  • Device Information: Basic device and technical information, such as device type, operating system, and app diagnostic information, where necessary for app functionality, security, and troubleshooting.
  • Login Information: Authentication details and related information required to verify your identity and provide secure access to your account.

We use this information to provide app features, display relevant milk-related records, maintain account access, improve app functionality, troubleshoot technical issues, and help ensure the security of our services.

2. Access to SmartX LTE Web Portal Data

The SmartX LTE mobile app allows users to access selected information and features associated with their SmartX LTE web portal accounts. The information displayed in the app depends on the user's account, access permissions, and the features made available in the mobile application.

Users are responsible for keeping their login credentials confidential and for accessing only the information they are authorized to view.

3. Data Storage and Security

User information and milk-related records may be stored on secure backend servers and processed through the SmartX LTE platform to provide the app's services.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, loss, or misuse. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information. We may share or disclose information only when necessary to:

  • Provide and maintain SmartX LTE services.
  • Process and display milk-related records and account activities.
  • Support hosting, maintenance, security, and technical operations.
  • Comply with applicable laws, regulations, or lawful requests.
  • Protect the rights, safety, and security of users and our services.

Where third-party service providers process information on our behalf, we take reasonable steps to ensure that the information is handled appropriately.

5. App Permissions

The SmartX LTE mobile app may require certain device permissions depending on the features available in the app. Any permission requested will be used for its intended functionality.

If a feature requires access to device storage, photos, notifications, or other device capabilities, the app will use those permissions only as needed for that feature. You can manage available permissions through your device settings.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information and milk-related records for as long as reasonably necessary to provide our services, maintain business records, meet operational requirements, and comply with applicable legal obligations.

Data may be retained for a longer period where required by law or where necessary to resolve disputes and protect our legitimate interests.

7. Children's Privacy

The SmartX LTE mobile app is intended for authorized users of the SmartX LTE platform and is not designed specifically for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13 through the app.

8. User Responsibilities

Users should keep their account credentials secure, log out when using shared devices where appropriate, and promptly report any suspected unauthorized access to their accounts.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our app features, data practices, or legal requirements. Updated versions will be published on the relevant app or website page. Significant changes may also be communicated through the app or by email, where appropriate.
