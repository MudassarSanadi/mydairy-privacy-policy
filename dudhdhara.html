Dudh Dhara

1. Introduction

Dudh Dhara respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how the Dudh Dhara mobile application handles user information when you use our services.

Dudh Dhara is a dairy milk collection and management application designed to help dairy owners manage farmer records, milk collection transactions, quantities, payments and related business activities.

2. Information Collection and Use

Dudh Dhara may allow users to enter and manage dairy-related information, including:

  • Farmer names and contact details.
  • Farmer mobile phone numbers.
  • Milk collection dates and times.
  • Milk quantities, fat and SNF details.
  • Milk rates and transaction amounts.
  • Payment details and dairy business records.

This information is used to provide milk collection management, transaction recording, farmer account management and related application features.

We do not sell personal information. Information is processed or shared only as necessary to provide application services, comply with legal obligations or protect our rights.

3. Data Storage and Security

Information entered into Dudh Dhara may be stored locally on the user's device or processed or stored on external servers, depending on the features used and application configuration.

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application against unauthorized access, misuse or disclosure.

However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

4. Permissions

Dudh Dhara may request access to certain Android device permissions when required to provide specific application features.

  • Internet: Used for network connectivity and supported online application services.
  • Bluetooth: Used to connect and communicate with supported Bluetooth devices.
  • Location: May be required for Bluetooth scanning on supported older Android versions.
  • SMS: Used to send milk collection transaction acknowledgement messages to farmers through the dairy owner's mobile SIM card.
  • Storage: Used for supported database backup and file management operations.

Permissions are requested when required for the relevant application functionality. Users can manage or revoke permissions through Android device settings.

5. Third-Party Services

Depending on the application features and configuration, Dudh Dhara may use third-party services such as cloud storage, hosting, authentication or other infrastructure services.

Such service providers may process information only as necessary to provide their respective services and according to their applicable privacy policies.

Direct SIM-based SMS notifications do not require a third-party SMS gateway or internet-based SMS delivery service.

6. Children's Privacy

Dudh Dhara is intended for dairy business and milk management purposes and is not specifically designed for children under the age of 13.

We do not knowingly design the application to collect personal information directly from children under 13.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may remain on the user's device or external servers for as long as necessary to provide the application services or meet applicable legal obligations.

9. SMS Transaction Notifications

Dudh Dhara provides an optional SMS notification feature to help dairy owners send immediate milk collection transaction acknowledgements to farmers.

How SMS Notifications Work

When a dairy owner saves a milk collection transaction and chooses to use the SMS notification feature, the application can send an acknowledgement message directly through the SMS service of the dairy owner's mobile network and SIM card.

The application uses the Android SMS functionality to send the message. An internet connection or third-party SMS gateway is not required for this direct SIM-based SMS functionality.

Information Used for SMS Notifications

Depending on the transaction and message content, the application may use:

  • Farmer's registered mobile number.
  • Farmer's name, where applicable.
  • Milk collection date and time.
  • Milk quantity.
  • Milk rate and transaction amount, where applicable.

Purpose of SMS Notifications

This information is used to prepare and send milk collection transaction acknowledgement messages to the concerned farmers.

The purpose is to improve transparency and communication between dairy owners and farmers by providing confirmation of recorded milk collection transactions.

SIM Card and Mobile Network

SMS messages are sent using the dairy owner's mobile SIM card and are subject to mobile network availability, SIM functionality, operator restrictions and applicable SMS charges.

SMS sending does not require the messaging application to be opened. However, successful sending does not necessarily guarantee delivery to the recipient.

Privacy and Data Usage

Farmer mobile numbers and transaction information used for SMS notifications are intended for the relevant milk collection acknowledgement purpose.

Dudh Dhara does not use this information for advertising, promotional SMS campaigns or unrelated marketing purposes.

Dairy owners are responsible for ensuring that they have appropriate authorization to use farmers' mobile numbers for transaction notifications.
